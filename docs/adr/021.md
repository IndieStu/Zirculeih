# ADR-021 — Month calendar, continuous selection and transition truth

2026-10-16. Owner explicitly rejects the free-slot-card direction and authorizes
replacement within the Phase-6 review correction, not Phase 7.

Month cells select dates. A selected slot-based day displays contiguous occupancy
segments on a visual timeline; accessible start/end controls select a continuous
interval and stay synchronized with the timeline. Day-based resources select an
inclusive date range on the month calendar. No per-grid free-slot cards.

The public projection preloads current units, claims, custody, blocks and the
existing schedule owners/exceptions. Boundaries are UTC instants, displayed locally;
DST days retain 23/25-hour lengths. Occupancy never includes booking identities.
Selections are validated server-side before continuation; frontend constraints and
feedback consume the same projected intervals. Final confirmation rechecks all
current personal requirements and transactional allocation.

Reuse BookingPolicy duration, granularity and before/after buffers. For current
onsite resources (rooms and machines), new policies default to 30 minutes after
use; total configured transition must be at least 30 minutes. Physical loans retain
independent configurable buffers and day semantics. No duplicate duration setting.

Existing immutable claims and bookings are not rewritten. Capacity checking also
considers current policy padding around existing promises, unioned with stored
claims, so increasing transition immediately protects both sides of new selections.
Pre-existing promises remain intact; conflicts under new review settings are flagged
for staff review, not cancelled. New claims retain PostgreSQL exclusion protection,
unit locks and the shared authorization transaction. Completed onsite booking
history continues to constrain the remaining transition, without reactivating an
immutable historical claim or changing Booking/Claim lifecycle consistency. An
additional deferred PostgreSQL trigger locks the unit and rejects transitions
against active promises or completed onsite history, including direct writes.
Physical returned loans keep their existing early-release behavior. No frontend
booking bypass.

An explicit additive review-calendar enhancement sets meaningful hours/durations,
room/machine transitions, closure/block examples and synthetic existing bookings.
Existing team records/passwords/media remain untouched. No reset or Phase 7.
